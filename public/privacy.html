Orbique PMS Privacy Policy

Orbique Global Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Orbique PMS is a property management platform for short-term rental operators. This policy explains what personal data the platform processes, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and your rights under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

Each operator that subscribes to Orbique PMS runs its own private workspace. For guest data in a workspace, the operator decides what is collected and why, and Orbique processes it on the operator's behalf.

What data we process

  • Account data: the name, email address and sign-in details of operators and their staff.
  • Workspace data: the operator's properties, rates, bookings, tasks, invoices, expenses, settings and branding.
  • Guest booking details: guest name, contact details, stay dates, number of guests and booking notes, received from connected booking sites or entered by the operator.
  • Guest messages: messages, photos, files and voice notes exchanged with guests through connected booking sites, WhatsApp and email, and their translations.
  • Identity documents: ID photos guests upload in the guest app for the check-in ID check.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to run and secure the service, such as request times and error reports.

Why we process it

  • To provide the platform: sign-in, workspaces, bookings, the calendar, the guest inbox and the guest app.
  • To send and receive guest messages on the operator's behalf, and to translate them when asked.
  • To send notifications the operator has switched on, such as new booking alerts.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us process it

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), hosted in Singapore.
  • Vercel (hosting of the app and its server functions).
  • Channex (channel manager connection to booking sites such as Booking.com and Airbnb).
  • Meta, WhatsApp Business Platform (sending and receiving WhatsApp messages).
  • Resend (sending and receiving email).
  • Google Firebase (push notifications on the phone app).
  • Anthropic (translation of messages, when the operator uses it).

Some of these providers process data outside Malaysia. We use them only for the purposes above, under their data protection terms.

How long we keep it

We keep workspace data, including guest bookings and messages, for as long as the operator's subscription is active, or until the operator deletes it. When a workspace is closed, its data is deleted within 90 days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to access your personal data, to correct it, and to withdraw consent or limit how it is processed. If you are a guest, please contact the operator you booked with first, as they manage your booking data. We will help them answer your request.

Data deletion

To ask for your data to be deleted, including data received through WhatsApp:

  • Guests: ask the operator you stayed with to delete your booking and messages. The operator can delete them in Orbique PMS, or pass the request to Orbique.
  • Operators and staff: the account owner can delete bookings, messages and team members in the workspace, or ask Orbique to close the workspace and delete all its data.
  • You can also send the request to Orbique Global directly (see Contact). Say which business you dealt with and the phone number or email you used. We confirm the deletion within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each workspace is kept separate, and staff screens follow the access the account owner gives each role.
