Channel manager

Every booking site. One calendar.

Connect Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia and Trip.com through the Channex channel manager. Orbique sends your availability, rates and restrictions and brings every booking into one timeline.

  • See 7, 14 or 30 days on a computer
  • Tap a bar to open the booking, tap a free night to add one
  • Block dates for owner stays and repairs

Inbox

Every guest chat. One chat per stay.

WhatsApp, booking-site chat and booking emails come into one inbox, next to the booking they belong to. Tap a dot to see what each part does.

  • Greeting message per building
  • Reply the way the guest wrote
  • Assign a chat to a teammate
  • New messages ring the alert chime

Guest app

A guest app for every booking.

Each booking gets its own link. Nothing to download, no sign-in. The guest checks in online and gets everything for the stay.

Door code shows 24 hours before check-in, once online check-in and the ID check are done
  • Online check-in and ID check (passport or MyKad)
  • Wi-Fi, house guide and house rules
  • Nearby places with maps that work offline
  • Requests and messages straight to your team

Booking alerts

Know the moment a booking lands.

New bookings, cancellations and date changes from every booking site reach your team 3 ways: email, WhatsApp and a push to the Orbique Android app (in testing). When Orbique is open on a screen, it also plays its own loud chime.

  • Rate plans to compare, each with its risk, pros and cons

Team

Everyone sees what their job needs.

Pick a role for each person, then fine-tune it: 52 switches across 20 sections. Cleaners do not see the money, accountants do not see the door codes. Financials and the profit planner stay with account owners.

Operations

The work behind every stay.

  • Tasks and cleaning

    Assign tasks to staff, with checklists for turnovers and daily housekeeping.

  • Financials

    Profit and loss, cash flow and each property's numbers, as PDF or CSV.

  • Invoices and expenses

    An invoice for every booking and a receipt when it is paid. Staff snap receipts and claim money back.

  • Reviews

    Reviews from your booking sites, a suggested reply for each, and which ones still need an answer.
